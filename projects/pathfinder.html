Reads your files

Text and titles in PDFs, dates in photos, titles in Office files, and text in screenshots and scans with Windows' built-in OCR.

Learns your habits

Move an invoice to "Accounts" and the next ones go there too. After three of the same choice it offers to do it automatically.

Cleans up

Finds duplicates, installers for programs you already have, unpacked zips and big files you haven't touched in six months. Everything goes to the Recycle Bin.

Undo anything

Ctrl+Z, the Undo button or the History page. Automatic sorting can be undone too.

Converts files

Images, audio, video and PDF, many at a time. The originals are never touched.

Builds a folder structure

No folders yet? Pathfinder suggests Finance, Home, Work, School and more, and reuses the folders you already have.
