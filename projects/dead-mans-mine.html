Dead Man's Mine

The miners went down in '81. Something else came up.

Third person1 to 3 playersWestern horror

The claim

Walk out of the desert yard and into a mine that should have stayed sealed. Your revolver holds six rounds and you reload them one at a time. Light is scarce, and so are bullets.

The Taken

The men who never came back up are still down here, standing at their posts. Some sleep. Some don't. Shove them back, line up the shot and keep your medical kit close.

Foreman's note, found on the gateThree pillars. Light them in the order the men died. Do not open this door after dark.

Controls

  • Move WASD
  • Aim / fire RMB / LMB
  • Reload R
  • Dodge Space
  • Shove V
  • Medical kit H

Full gamepad support.
