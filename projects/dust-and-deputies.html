Dust & Deputies

A Sheriff for Dustfall

Build your defenses by day, hold them by night, and get out with the loot before the desert takes you.

By day

Build

Explore a compact map around the dying railway town of Dustfall. Clear outposts, gather what you can carry and raise sentries, barricades and traps around the Core.

  • Watchtowers, barricades, traps
  • Named deputies, each with one clear job
Every few days

Extract

A vehicle rolls in. Defend it while you load the loot, then decide how much risk the next night is worth.

By night

Hold the line

Bandits and worse come in waves. Fight from the top-down view with your revolver while your defenses do their part.
